Privacy Policy

Personal Event Feed is a personal tool used only by its author.

Google data accessed

  • Google Calendar: creates, updates and reads events on a calendar it manages.
  • Gmail: reads messages carrying a specific label, changes their labels, and inserts a weekly digest message into the inbox.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored only on the author's own computer. To extract event details, the contents of labelled messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude through the author's own Claude subscription. Data is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
